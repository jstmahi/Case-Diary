**PROJECT:**
**Forest-Fire-Detection-Using-MSA-Net** [[https://github.com/jstmahi/Forest-Fire-Detection](https://github.com/jstmahi/Forest-Fire-Detection)]

Engineered a lightweight, real-time deep learning pipeline to automate the early detection of forest fires, designed specifically for deployment on resource-constrained edge devices such as drones and IoT sensors.

* **Custom Neural Network Architecture:** Designed a Multi-Scale Attention Network (MSA-Net) utilizing varying filter sizes (3x3, 5x5, 7x7) to capture both fine-grained and broad contextual visual features.
* **Attention Mechanisms:** Implemented Squeeze-and-Excitation (SE) blocks for dynamic channel-wise feature recalibration, allowing the model to focus on critical fire indicators while suppressing background environmental noise.
* **Data Engineering & Image Processing:** Curated and augmented a comprehensive fire/non-fire image dataset. Applied advanced preprocessing techniques—including normalization, contrast enhancement, and Gaussian smoothing—to mitigate dataset imbalance and handle complex lighting conditions.
* **Model Optimization & Performance:** Achieved a highly robust 96.1% test accuracy and an AUC-ROC of 0.96. Utilized early stopping and learning rate scheduling during training to prevent overfitting and successfully minimized false positive rates (Precision: 96.5%, Recall: 96%).
* **Cross-Functional Collaboration:** Collaborated within a 4-member team under faculty guidance, managing the end-to-end workflow from data acquisition to final deployment readiness, and translating technical findings into stakeholder-friendly visualizations.
